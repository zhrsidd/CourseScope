# CourseScope v1.1.0 — Tuition fees: release report

*Prepared 1 October 2026. Not tagged: tagging waits for live production verification.*

## Addendum, 1 October 2026 — Southampton HH72/H722 withdrawn

Before tagging, the two Southampton cases flagged below under "Unresolved cases" — HH72 and H722,
whose pages said *"This course is not open to applicants for 2027 entry"* — were investigated.
Both official pages were re-read and confirmed the banner still stands. HH72 (Acoustical
Engineering BEng) had been carried here as a fully verified 2027 course with a published AAB
offer; H722 (MEng) was an identity-only awaiting-data shell. Neither is a 2027 application
Southampton is currently accepting, and the schema has no status for "identity confirmed,
application confirmed not running this cycle" that is weaker than removal without implying the
application is merely pending. Both records are withdrawn from the 2027 catalogue entirely (HH72's
derived 2028 "not yet published" shell goes with it). Full reasoning and the quoted evidence are in
`src/data/courses.real.batch6.southampton.ts`.

Catalogue counts changed as a direct, intended result: **1,132 → 1,129** served applications
(563 → 562 verified; 569 → 567 for 2027; 563 → 562 for 2028). No other course, fee row, or
eligibility behaviour changed. Regression fixtures (search counts, the two pinned eligibility-verdict
hashes, and the catalogue requirement-set hash) were updated to match in `engine-check.ts`, and the
full suite was rerun — see the updated Tests table below. This was not treated as an invitation to
re-review the other unresolved cases in this report; none of them were touched.

## What shipped

- A compact **Tuition fees** section on every course page, in its own card after all admissions
  cards, stating that fees are information only and play no part in eligibility.
- A **Tuition fees** row in Compare. No fee filters; nothing added to course cards.
- A new `TuitionFee` relation (one row per published category, per course record), separate from
  course records. No course record changed; records without fees remain valid.
- Validation rules and regression tests for year leakage, missing provenance, duplicate categories,
  Scottish multi-category fees, and course-specific vs university-wide fees.

## Unresolved cases

Every one of these is shown to students as *not yet confirmed*, *not established* or *not
recorded* — never as a current fee.

- **Southampton (25 courses researched for fees, nothing recorded).** Course pages and the fee
  table give figures with no entry year; one page is still on 2026/27; BB97 shows £31,000 on its
  page but £33,500 in the table. **HH72 and H722**, the two cases whose pages said “This course is
  not open to applicants for 2027 entry,” were withdrawn from the 2027 catalogue in the addendum
  above; the admissions question is resolved. The other 23 Southampton courses' fee gap is
  unaffected and remains as described here.
- **Leeds (15 courses, nothing recorded).** Course pages served earlier cycles (2021–2026 start
  dates) or returned 403. 13 further Leeds courses have UK published and International “To be
  confirmed”.
- **Sheffield.** Overseas unknown for all 49: only a range (£27,650–£34,670) is published and the
  per-course look-up could not be read. Home unknown for the 22 foundation-year courses: year 1 is
  “either £10,050 … or £5,760” without saying which course pays which.
- **Warwick Overseas (24).** Conflict: course pages say 2027-28 overseas fees “have not been set”;
  the Student Finance table lists £37,310. Not resolved by picking one.
- **Birmingham.** International fees are not published per course. Course pages disagree on Home:
  12 show £10,050 for 2027/28, 43 say “To be confirmed”. Physics with Medical Physics BSc page
  would not load.
- **Durham (19).** Home only as an expected £10,050 “subject to parliamentary approval”; Overseas
  “not yet confirmed”.
- **York (5: H634, H640, H641, H116, H221).** Course pages say TBC while the UK fees page states
  £10,050. The other 42 York courses show UK as expected (“We expect this to apply”).
- **Glasgow.** Rest of UK “TBC”; International given only as “expected” (£33,708, labelled as

- **UCL.** UK is £10,050 “subject to parliamentary approval” on course pages (recorded as

- **Imperial (2: Mechanical, Aeronautical MEng).** Fees section not retrievable.
- **Lancaster (3: F3FM, F3F5, F3F9).** Not read — rate-limited.
- **KCL International (4).** Not retrievable from official pages.
- **Manchester MMath&Phys** (no international figure) and **Nottingham Foundation H100**

## Method and verification

- Research was done per university from official pages only, following a written brief
  (no inference, year evidence quoted verbatim, conflicts → unknown). Raw results with full notes:
  `data/research/fees-2027/`. The app data is generated from them by
  `scripts/import-fee-research.mjs`; an assertion checks the two match row for row.
- Several sites served stale cached copies to plain fetches (Imperial, Lancaster, Manchester,
  Bristol, St Andrews); every figure was re-read from a fresh copy.
- Independent spot checks against live pages: Imperial Physics BSc, QMUL Physics, Manchester
  Physics BSc, Nottingham Mathematical Physics, Cambridge Engineering, Oxford Engineering Science and
  Bath — all matched.
