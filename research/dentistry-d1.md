## Birmingham: Dental Surgery BDS (A200)
- https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/subjects/dentistry-courses/dental-surgery-bds — "Dental Surgery BDS - University of Birmingham". "UCAS code A200", "Start date September 2027", "Duration 5 years".
- "AAA to include Chemistry and Biology". Contextual Offer "AAB to include Chemistry and Biology"; Pathways to Birmingham "ABB to include Chemistry and Biology".
- GCSE: "GCSE required subjects to include A*/8 in Biology and Chemistry and A/7 in Mathematics and English Language or Literature. GCSE resits are not considered."
- Age 18 by 1 October of entry. "15 October 2026" deadline. "Sit the University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT) in year of application" "Attend interview if invited".

## Bristol: BDS Dentistry (A206)
- https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/2027/dentistry/bds-dentistry/ — "BDS Dentistry | Study at Bristol | University of Bristol". "2027 entry", "UCAS (Code: A206)", "5 year(s) full-time".
- "AAA including Chemistry and one of Biology, Physics, Mathematics or Further Mathematics." Contextual: "ABB including A in Chemistry and B in one of Biology, Physics, Mathematics or Further Mathematics."
- GCSE: "Advanced numeracy requirement (7 or A in GCSE Mathematics or equivalent) and Standard literacy requirement (4 or C in GCSE English or equivalent)".
- UCAT: "The combined score from all subtests, with the exception of Situational Judgement, will be used to select applicants for interview. For 2026 entry, the final UCAT threshold score to be invited to interview was 2340 for home applicants and 1970 for overseas applicants."
- "Applicants must be available for an interview from November to April".

## Sheffield: Dental Surgery BDS (A200)
- https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/undergraduate/courses/2027/dental-surgery-bds — "Dental Surgery BDS | Undergraduate study | The University of Sheffield". "UCAS code: A200", 5 years.
- "AAA including Chemistry and Biology + pass in the practical element of any science A Levels taken". "Human Biology is acceptable in lieu of Biology". Access Sheffield offer: "AAB including Chemistry and Biology". EPQ alternative AAB (AA in Chemistry and Biology + B in EPQ), firm choice only.
- GCSE: "6 GCSEs at grade 7/A including English, Maths and Science (Chemistry and Biology or dual science)".
