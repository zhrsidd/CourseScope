## Structural surprises (things a reasonable person would get wrong)
1. **Two-column offer structure.** Every non-foundation course publishes a **"Standard Offer"** and an **"Access Sheffield Offer"** side by side. The Access Sheffield offer is Sheffield's contextual offer and is recorded separately throughout below. It is typically **one grade lower**, but **not always** — see item 3.
2. **Foundation-year courses have NO Access Sheffield column at all.** Every one of the 12 engineering foundation-year courses and both physics foundation-year courses publishes a single offer only. This is a genuine structural difference, not a retrieval failure.
3. **Mechanical Engineering's Access offer restructures the subject rule, not just the grades.** Standard is `A*AA including Maths and at least one of Physics, Chemistry or Biology`; Access Sheffield is `AAB including A in Maths and B in at least one of Physics, Chemistry or Biology` — i.e. it *adds* per-subject minimum grades that the standard offer does not state. Do not model this as a simple grade decrement.
4. **The EPQ / fourth-Level-3 row is IDENTICAL in both columns** on every course that has it. The EPQ route lands at the same grades as the Access Sheffield offer, so contextual applicants get no further reduction via EPQ. Verified independently on 15+ pages.
5. **Foundation-year offers are a dual-format string: `BBB; BBC`.** This is not a range and not a typo. It expands to **"BBB (any A Level); BBC including Maths and at least one of Physics, Chemistry or Biology"** — two alternative routes in one cell. Recorded verbatim below.
6. **Physics uses "Maths and Physics"; physics foundation year uses "Maths and/or Physics".** Different rule, one word apart.
7. **Civil / Architectural are the only courses that name subjects as NOT accepted**: *"but not Statistics or Use of Maths"*, plus a conditional: *"Pure Maths is only acceptable when combined with Physics"*.
8. **One UCAS code covering several named degrees:**
   - **General Engineering** (H103 / H100) — a single application leading to **six named streams** chosen after admission (Biomedical; Civil & Structural; Electrical & Software; Energy & Sustainability; Mechanical & Aerospace; General).
   - **Mechanical Engineering MEng** (H300) — **Route A** (Mechanical Engineering) and **Route B** (Mechanical Engineering with Biomechanics) within one code.
   - **Biomedical Engineering** (H673 / H675) — four named final-year specialisms.
   - **Aerospace Engineering** (H402 / H400) — Avionics and Aeromechanics streams.
9. **Civil and Structural is asymmetric across award levels.** BEng is titled *Civil Engineering* (H202); MEng is titled *Civil and Structural Engineering* (H210). There is **no Civil Engineering MEng** and **no Civil and Structural Engineering BEng**. The foundation-year pair mirrors this (H209 BEng *Civil Engineering*; H201 MEng *Civil and Structural*).
10. **Computer Systems Engineering MEng carries UCAS code G500** — a computing-style code paired with BEng H130. Verified twice on the page itself; it is not a transcription slip.
11. **Year in industry and year abroad are STUDY OPTIONS everywhere, never separate UCAS codes.** No "with a Year in Industry" or "with a Year Abroad" course has its own code in the 2027 catalogue. Recorded under field 19 per course.
12. **No admissions test and no interview is stated on ANY of the 49 pages.** Recorded as *not stated* — the pages are silent, which is not the same as an explicit "no test". No Physics or Engineering course at Sheffield references PAT, ESAT, TMUA, MAT or any other test.
13. **GCSE requirements vary sharply and are easy to mis-copy**: Physics degrees state only an English Language requirement; Civil/Architectural add *GCSE Physics (or Combined Science) grade 6/B*; Materials uses *GCSE Maths grade 6/B and 4/C in Physics and Chemistry (if not studied at A Level)*; all foundation years use *GCSE Science grade 6/B (or 65 in GCSE Double Award Science) and Maths grade 7/A*, but **conditionally waived**.
14. **Reduced-offer routes beyond the course pages.** `https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/study/policies/access-sheffield` documents **Access Sheffield** as the umbrella contextual-admissions policy, with a named sub-scheme **Access+** covering care-experienced students, care leavers, young carers, estranged students, student parents, forced migrants (asylum seekers/refugees), free-school-meals recipients and IMD quintile 1–2 residents; plus recognised widening-access programmes (Realising Opportunities, Access to Sheffield Y12–13, Access to Sheffield Summer School, Sutton Trust Summer School). Home fee status is required. The policy states A-Level contextual offers may be *"one, two or three grades below the standard requirements"* — note this is **wider than the one-grade reduction actually printed on the Physics/Engineering course pages**.

## Methodology / reliability caveat
Direct HTTP access to `sheffield.ac.uk` was blocked by the environment's egress proxy, so every page was read through the fetch tool, which renders the page and transcribes it. Grade strings, UCAS codes and subject wording below are as that transcription reported them. Where a value looked structurally odd (General Engineering MEng; Computer Systems Engineering MEng's G500; the repeated EPQ rows) it was re-fetched with a cache-busting query string to confirm. Values were additionally cross-checked against the departmental listing page for Physics, which independently reported the same UCAS codes and headline grades for all 8 physics courses.

# APPENDIX A — REDUCED-OFFER ROUTES (field 15, university-wide)

Source: `https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/study/policies/access-sheffield` — page heading **"Access Sheffield policies (Contextual admissions)"**.

**Route name as the university prints it: "Access Sheffield Offer"** (this is the column heading on every course page).

- **What it is:** a contextual offer set below the standard requirement. The policy page states A-Level contextual offers may be *"one, two or three grades below the standard requirements"*, with a worked example: a standard offer of AAB with A in Maths could become ABB with A in Maths. **On the Physics and Engineering course pages the printed reduction is consistently one grade** (except Mechanical, which also restructures the subject rule — see course 46/47).
- **Named sub-scheme: "Access+"** — provides additional support alongside the contextual offer.
- **Access+ eligibility groups (as listed):** residents of deprived areas (IMD quintiles 1–2); students entitled to free school meals; **care-experienced students**; **care leavers**; **young carers**; **estranged students**; students with parenting responsibilities; **forced migrants (asylum seekers, refugees, humanitarian protection recipients)**.
- **Widening-access programme routes:** Realising Opportunities completers; Access to Sheffield (Y12–13) participants; Access to Sheffield Summer School attendees; Sutton Trust Summer School attendees.
- **Qualification-based routes:** applicants with an **EPQ**, **Core Maths**, or **Further Maths** are named in the same policy.
- **Restriction:** applicants *"must have home fee paying status"* to benefit, with separate terms for forced migrants.
- Sub-page for detail: `https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/access-sheffield/plus/eligibility` (not separately fetched).

**Care-experienced / estranged students are covered inside Access+ rather than as a separate published offer type.** No distinct third offer column appears on any course page.
