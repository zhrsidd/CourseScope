## Birmingham: Medicine and Surgery MBChB (A100)
- https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/subjects/medicine-courses/medicine-and-surgery-mbchb
  — "Medicine and Surgery MBChB - University of Birmingham"
- "UCAS code A100", "Start date September 2027", "Duration 5 years", "Award Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery".
- "AAA with predicted AAB including Chemistry, and a second science from Biology/Human Biology, Physics or Maths"
- "A levels must include Chemistry and a second science from Biology/Human Biology, Physics or Maths. You must be
  predicted a minimum of AAB. ... General Studies, Critical Thinking and EPQ are not accepted as a third A level."
- GCSE: "English Language at grade 6/B or higher, Mathematics ... at grade 6/B or higher, Biology and Chemistry or
  dual award science at grade 6/B or higher."
- Contextual: "Contextual Offer AAB to include Chemistry and a second science from Biology/Human Biology, Physics or
  Maths." Pathways to Birmingham: "ABB to include Chemistry and a second science ...".
- Age: "must have reached the age of 18 years on 1 October of the year of entry."
- "15 October 2026 Application deadline for September 2027 entry." "Sit the University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT)
  in year of application" "Attend interview if invited".

## Leeds: Medicine and Surgery MBChB (A100)
- https://courses.leeds.ac.uk/5580/medicine-and-surgery-mbchb — "Medicine and Surgery MBChB | University of Leeds"
- "Year of entry 2027", "UCAS code A100", "Start date September 2027", "Duration 5 Years (Full time)".
- "A-level: AAA including Chemistry and Biology. ... an A* cannot compensate for a B in another subject, so we will
  not accept A*A*B instead of AAA. Certain combinations are not acceptable, specifically: Chemistry with Biology
  and Human Biology; Chemistry with Biology and Sports Science; Chemistry with Biology and Critical Thinking. EPQs
  are not considered within our scoring."
- GCSE: "A minimum of 6 GCSEs at grade 6 or above are required including Maths, English Language, Biology and
  Chemistry or Dual Science." One resit per qualification is accepted "From 2026 entry onwards".
- UCAT: "All applicants for the MBChB course are required to take UCAT. This test must be taken in the year of
  application." "There is no fixed UCAT cut-off score."
- Interview: "For students applying for 2027 entry, face-to-face interviews ... will take place on 4th, 5th and 6th
  January 2027 ... Using a multiple mini-interview format".
- Contextual: Access to Leeds "may be able to give priority"; no reduced A-level offer is printed.
- Deadline: "by 15 October of the year preceding entry" (no year printed).
- Modelling: the three excluded third-subject combinations are kept in notes, like other universities' excluded
  subjects; the engine does not enforce them.

## Sheffield: Medicine MBChB (A100)
- https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/undergraduate/courses/2027/medicine-mbchb — "Medicine MBChB | Undergraduate study |
  The University of Sheffield". "2027-28 entry", "UCAS code A100", 5 years.
- "AAA including Chemistry or Biology and a second science + pass in the practical element of any science A Levels taken"
- "Second science subjects include Biology/Human Biology, Chemistry, Maths, Physics or Psychology". "We do not accept
  A Level Further Maths when determining whether an applicant meets our A Level academic threshold."
- Alternative: "AAB, including Chemistry or Biology (at grade A) and a second science + A in the EPQ ... subject to
  you making the University of Sheffield your Firm choice".
- Contextual (Access Sheffield): "AAB including Chemistry or Biology (at grade A) and a second science".
- GCSE: "5 GCSEs at grade 7/A, with at least grade 6/B in Maths, English Language and the science subjects".
- UCAT: "Each year, we set a minimum UCAT score threshold, which is 1800/2700 for 2027 entry." SJT reviewed only for
  those invited to interview. Interview: "Multiple mini-interviews (MMIs)".
- Modelling: the EPQ alternative depends on an EPQ grade the profile cannot hold, so it is in notes, not a pathway.

  January through to the end of February".
