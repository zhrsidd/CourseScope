## SUMMARY

### Scope

**47 courses in scope** — 25 Physics-family, 22 Engineering-family. All were located from York's own
index pages, cross-checked against three independent official listings:

1. <https://www.york.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/courses/> (central A–Z, 2027/28)
2. <https://www.york.ac.uk/physics-engineering-technology/study/> (School of Physics, Engineering and Technology)
3. Targeted site-scoped search on `york.ac.uk` to catch titles sorting outside a "Physics"/"Engineering" prefix

### ⚠️ Listing discrepancy between York's own two index pages

The School (SPET) study page and the central A–Z do **not** list the same set. Two courses appear on
the central A–Z but are **missing from the School's own study page**:

- **Engineering (with a Foundation Year), BEng, H100**
- **Engineering with Renewable Energy, MEng, H221**

Conversely the School page is the only place that groups **Mathematics and Physics** (3 courses) with
the Physics degrees; those sort under "M" in the central A–Z and are easy to miss. The union of both
lists is used here. This is a genuine inconsistency in York's own published material, not a
research artefact.

### The single most important structural finding

**York does not offer Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, Aerospace/Aeronautical, Automotive, Materials,
Manufacturing, Mining, Petroleum, Structural or General Civil-adjacent engineering degrees.**
See the **ENGINEERING: WHAT EXISTS** section below for the full honest answer.

### Structural surprises (detail in body)

- **"Mathematical Physics" does not exist at York.** The nearest genuine programme is
  **Mathematics and Physics** (BSc GF13, and MMath/MPhys), a joint degree with the Department of
  Mathematics. Recorded as its own course, NOT relabelled.
- **Physics with Philosophy exists** and is genuinely offered (BSc F3V5, MPhys F3VM, BSc year
  abroad F3V7) — but there is **no year-in-industry variant** of it and **no MPhys year-abroad**
  variant. The variant grid is not symmetric across Physics streams.
- **The BSc/MPhys offer split is a full grade:** BSc Physics is **AAB**, MPhys Physics is **AAA**.
  These are different and are never normalised in this document.
- **Several engineering titles exist at only one award level** — Robotic Engineering and Medical
  Engineering are **MEng-only**; Biomedical Engineering and Intelligent Digital Health Engineering
  are **BEng-only**; Engineering with Renewable Energy is **MEng-only**. A reader expecting a
  BEng/MEng pair for every title will be wrong.
- **A fourth alternative-offer row, "The York Tutorial Programme", appears on some course pages but
  not others.** Recorded per course exactly where seen.

### Entry-year status

Recorded per course in field 20. One page (**F306, MPhys Physics with a year in industry**) returned
an entry-year label of **2026/27** rather than 2027/28 — flagged in its own section and NOT treated
as 2027 evidence.

### Cross-cutting: admissions tests, interviews, GCSEs, practical endorsement

For every Physics and Engineering course page read, York's course pages contain **no statement at
all** about an admissions test, an interview, a GCSE requirement, the science practical endorsement,
or a Further Mathematics requirement. Per the source policy this is recorded as **NOT PUBLISHED /
NOT STATED on the course page** — it is explicitly **NOT** recorded as "no admissions test" or "no
interview", because silence is not a denial. Where a central page adds anything, it is quoted and
attributed to that central page, not to the course page.

## ENGINEERING: WHAT EXISTS — the honest answer

**Question asked:** does York offer the broad engineering portfolio a reader would expect from a
Sheffield or a Leeds, and if not, what is actually there?

**Answer: no, and the gap is large and structural, not incidental.**

York's engineering provision sits inside a single school — the **School of Physics, Engineering and
Technology (SPET)** — and is built almost entirely around **electronics, electrical systems,
computing, audio/music technology, and medical/biomedical devices**. It is, in origin and in
substance, an *electronics* department that has broadened into adjacent applied areas, plus a
general "Engineering" pathway. It is **not** a full-spectrum engineering faculty.

### Disciplines a reader would reasonably expect — and which are simply ABSENT

Each of the following was checked against York's own central A–Z course listing and the School's own
study page. **None of these degrees exists at York for 2027 entry:**

| Expected discipline | Status at York |
|---|---|
| **Mechanical Engineering** | **DOES NOT EXIST** — no BEng, no MEng, no variant |
| **Civil Engineering** | **DOES NOT EXIST** |
| **Chemical Engineering** | **DOES NOT EXIST** |
| **Aerospace / Aeronautical Engineering** | **DOES NOT EXIST** |
| **Automotive Engineering** | **DOES NOT EXIST** |
| **Materials Science / Engineering** | **DOES NOT EXIST** |
| **Manufacturing / Structural / Mining / Petroleum Engineering** | **DOES NOT EXIST** |
| **Mechatronics** (under that name) | **DOES NOT EXIST under that title.** The nearest is **Robotic Engineering (MEng, H659)** — a different title and recorded as such, not as a Mechatronics substitute |
| **Electrical Engineering** (as a standalone single-discipline title) | **DOES NOT EXIST standalone.** Electrical appears only as half of **Electronic and Electrical Engineering** (BEng H600 / MEng H606) |

**The absence of these disciplines is a finding, not a retrieval failure.** They were searched for in
York's own index and are not there. No figures have been manufactured for them anywhere in this
document.

### What York's engineering provision actually consists of

Seven title families, in 22 UCAS-coded courses:

1. **Engineering** (the general/broad pathway) — BEng H105, MEng H109, BEng with Foundation Year H100
2. **Electronic Engineering** — BEng H610, MEng H609, BEng with Foundation Year H604
3. **Electronic and Electrical Engineering** — BEng H600, MEng H606
4. **Electronic and Computer Engineering** — BEng H634, MEng H639
5. **Audio / Music Technology cluster** — Music Technology Systems (BEng H663, MEng H666, BEng with
   Foundation Year H662); Electronic Engineering with Music Technology Systems (BEng H667, MEng H669);
   Engineering with Audio Technology (BEng H640, MEng H641)
6. **Medical / health cluster** — Biomedical Engineering (BEng H160, BEng-only); Medical Engineering
   (MEng H119, MEng-only); Intelligent Digital Health Engineering (BEng H116, BEng-only)
7. **Robotics and energy** — Robotic Engineering (MEng H659, MEng-only); Engineering with Renewable
   Energy (MEng H221, MEng-only)

### On the Physics/Engineering merger and the application structure

York merged Physics and Electronic Engineering into the **School of Physics, Engineering and
Technology**. The question of whether that merger changed the *application* structure — a common
first year, or a single Engineering code with later specialisation — was tested directly against the
School's own pages.

**Finding: the merger did NOT collapse the application structure.** Applicants still apply to a
specific named UCAS code, and Physics and Engineering remain separately coded and separately
recruited. The School's own study page presents them as two distinct subject groupings. The only
statement on the School page touching on cross-disciplinarity is:

> "You'll be supported as you explore your choice of single- or multi-disciplinary topics, from pure
> physics to practical engineering and the many significant places where the two intersect."

That is prospectus language about module choice, **not** evidence of a common first year or of a
single Engineering code with later specialisation. **No common-first-year statement and no
later-specialisation mechanism was found published on the School page** — recorded as
**NOT PUBLISHED**, not as "does not exist". The general **Engineering (BEng H105 / MEng H109)**
pathway is the closest thing York has to a broad-entry-then-specialise route, and its own course page
is the evidence for what it actually permits — see its section below.
