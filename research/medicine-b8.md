## Imperial College London: Medicine MBBS/BSc (A100)

- URL: https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/courses/undergraduate/2027/medicine/ (served at
  https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/courses/undergraduate/medicine/ after redirect)
- Page title: "Medicine MBBS/BSc | Study | Imperial College London"
- Key facts: "Qualification MBBS/BSc", "Duration 6 years", "Start date October 2027",
  "UCAS course code A100".
- Minimum entry standard (key facts): "A*AA including Chemistry and Biology, with A* in one of these
  subjects (A-levels)"
- A-levels: "Minimum entry standard A*AA To include: A* and A in Biology and Chemistry (A* in either)
  A in a third subject For contextual offers, the A* can be in any subject. All qualifications must be
  taken in the same sitting. Not accepted: General Studies and Critical Thinking Science Practical
  Endorsement: If you are made an offer you will be required to achieve a pass in the practical
  endorsement in all science subjects that form part of the offer."
- Typical offer: "A*AA (A* in Biology or Chemistry)"
- UCAT: "You will need to take the University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT) to study this course. You
  must book the test yourself and must only take it once in an admissions cycle. It must be taken in
  the year that you apply." "UCAT scores are used to help decide shortlisting for interview but are
  not the sole factor." "For 2026 entry, the following UCAT thresholds were applied: UCAT Threshold for
  Home Applicants SJT: Band 3 minimum (Band 4 discounted) Minimum threshold Total Score: 2320 UCAT
  Thresholds for Contextual Applicants SJT: Band 3 minimum (Band 4 discounted) Minimum threshold total
  Score: 2170". These are 2026-entry figures and are recorded as such, not as 2027 thresholds.
- Interview: "No offers are made without applicants attending a competitive interview." "If you are
  shortlisted, you will be invited to take part in Multiple Mini Interviews (MMIs)."
- GCSE: not stated on the page.
- Deadline: "Application deadlines – Thursday 15 October 2026 at 18.00 (UK time)". The page also says
  "Applications for this course are now closed."

## King's College London: Medicine MBBS (A100)

- Course page: https://www.kcl.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/courses/medicine-mbbs, title
  "Medicine MBBS | King's College London". Key information: "UCAS code: A100", "Required A-Levels:
  A*AA", "Duration: Five years (six if an optional intercalated degree is undertaken)",
  "Start date: September 2027". No "applies to 2026 entry" disclaimer on either page.
- Entry requirements: https://www.kcl.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/courses/medicine-mbbs/entry-requirements,
  title "Medicine - Entry Requirements - Medicine MBBS | King's College London".
- A-levels: "Required subjects: A-level (or equivalent) grade A in Chemistry and Biology Preferred
  subjects: No preferred subjects" "Required grades: A*AA Must include grade A in Biology and
  Chemistry. Please note that A-level General Studies, Critical Thinking, Thinking Skills and Global
  Perspectives are not accepted by King's as one of your A-levels. If you are taking linear A-levels in
  England, you will be required to pass the practical endorsement in all Science subjects. ... We do not
  consider the EPQ at any point of the assessment process. All applicants must take the UCAT."
- Contextual: "Contextual requirements Not specified." and "Please note, we do not make contextual
  offers for this programme and applicants eligible for contextualised admissions should consider
  applying to our Extended Medical Degree Programme MBBS (A101)."
- GCSE: "GCSE grade 6/B in both English Language and Mathematics is required."
- UCAT: "All applicants are required to sit the University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT) prior to
  applying to this programme." "For the most recent admissions cycle, applications were assessed using
  a ranking process that considered UCAT performance, GCSE grades and widening participation
  indicators. No minimum UCAT threshold score was applied." "The overall UCAT score is considered as
  part of the shortlisting process. Individual UCAT subtest scores are not considered separately, and
  the Situational Judgement Test (SJT) is not taken into account when shortlisting."
- Interview: "No offers are made without an interview. Interviews are usually held between December
  and April ... During the current Admissions cycle, interviews will be conducted remotely."
- Age: "King's is only able to accept applicants who will turn 18 years of age before commencing the
  second year of the Medicine degree."
- Deadline: the "Application deadline:" field in Key Information is empty. Not recorded.

## University of Manchester: MBChB Medicine (A106)

- URL: https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/courses/2027/01428/mbchb-medicine/
- Page title: "MBChB Medicine (2027 entry) | The University of Manchester"
- Key facts: "Duration: 5 years", "Year of entry: 2027", "UCAS course code: A106 / Institution code:
  M20". Award: "Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB)".
- Summary: "Typical A-level offer: AAA (including specific subjects)", "Typical contextual A-level offer:
  AAB including specific subjects", "Care experienced/sanctuary/carer offer: ABB including specific
  subjects".
- A-levels: "AAA, including Biology/Human Biology or Chemistry; and One of the following subjects from
  Chemistry; Biology/Human Biology; Physics; Psychology; Mathematics or Further Mathematics. As long as
  applicants meet our science subject requirements we have no preference for the third subject. A
  combination of three sciences is equally acceptable at A-level. However, our offer will not include
  combinations of very similar subjects, for example, Biology and Human Biology or Maths and Further
  Maths together."
- Contextual: "AAB including in: Biology/Human Biology or Chemistry; and One of the following subjects
  from Chemistry; Biology/Human Biology; Physics; Psychology; Mathematics or Further Mathematics."
- GCSE: "We require at least seven GCSEs at grade A (7) or A* (8+). Applicants must demonstrate a
  broad general education, including GCSE/IGCSE English Language, Mathematics and at least two science
  subjects, at a minimum of grade 6 (B). ... If Dual Award Science or Core and Additional Science are
  offered, the minimum required is BB (66)." "For applicants whose status has been confirmed as WP+ or
  WP++ ... we will accept 6 GCSEs at grade 7 (A) or above."
- UCAT: "Applicants are required to sit the University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT) in the year they
  wish to make their application." "Please note that we do not consider applicants who achieve Band 3
  or 4 in the Situational Judgement Test (SJT) element of the UCAT."
- Interview: "We do not make any offers without interview."
- Resits: "applicants may only re-sit one of either Year 12 or Year 13" ... "All re-sitting applicants
  are required to achieve Grades A*A*A in the A-level subjects specified above".
- Modelling note: the "very similar subjects" exclusion cannot be checked from grades alone, so it is
  recorded as a manual-review condition. The care-experienced ABB offer is recorded in the contextual
  details only: its own eligibility criteria are not on this page.

## University of Bristol: MB ChB Medicine (A100)

- URL: https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/2027/medicine/mb-chb-medicine/
- Page title: "MB ChB Medicine | Study at Bristol | University of Bristol"
- Heading: "MB ChB Medicine (A100)" "2027 entry". "Programme duration 5 year(s) full-time."
- A-levels: "A-level standard offer AAA including Chemistry and either Biology, Physics, Mathematics or
  Further Mathematics. Graduates are required to obtain a 2:1 in their degree plus BBB at A-level,
  including Chemistry and either Biology, Physics, Mathematics or Further Mathematics."
- Contextual: "A-level contextual offer ABB including A in Chemistry and B in one of Biology, Physics,
  Mathematics or Further Mathematics"
- GCSE: "Advanced numeracy requirement (7 or A in GCSE Mathematics or equivalent) and Standard
  literacy requirement (4 or C in GCSE English or equivalent)"
- UCAT: "The University Clinical Aptitude Test (formerly UKCAT) is required. ... The combined score from
  all subtests, with the exception of Situational Judgement, will be used to select applicants for
  interview. For 2026 entry, the final UCAT threshold score to be invited to interview was 2240 for
  Home applicants and 2270 for Overseas applicants."
- Interview: "Applicants must be available for an interview from November to April immediately
  following the submission of their application."
- Age: "Applicants must be 18 years of age (or above) on 1 September of the year that they enter the
  undergraduate medical programme."
- Deadline: "The closing date for Medicine UCAS applications is 15 October." No year is printed with
  it, so it is kept in the course notes and not entered as a dated deadline.
