## Newcastle: Medicine and Surgery MB BS (A100)
- https://www.ncl.ac.uk/undergraduate/degrees/a100/ — "Medicine and Surgery MB BS | Undergraduate | Newcastle University"
- "2027 entry information", "Full time: 5 years", "UCAS code A100", "Next start date September 2027",
  "Typical entry requirements A-Level: AAA". "the closing date for applications is 15 October 2026".
- "Through one of our contextual or alternative offer routes, you could receive an offer of up to three grades lower
  than the typical requirements."
- UCAT: "All applicants applying to study the 5 year A100 programme ... will be required to take the ... UCAT. The
  UCAT threshold may differ in each admissions cycle". Age: "turn 18 by the end of their first semester".
- 2027 Medicine Admissions Policy (PDF linked from the course page,
  https://www.ncl.ac.uk/mediav8/fms/files/2027%20Admissions%20Policy%20FINAL.pdf): "A level subjects should include
  Biology or Chemistry plus one of Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Statistics or Physics. For
  Biology, Chemistry and Physics A levels, a pass in the practical element is required." "For students entering with
  A levels this is typically AAA, ABB for contextual offer students or through one of our supported entry
  programmes." "Interviews for Home A100 and A101 applicants generally take the form of Multiple Mini Interviews
  (MMIs) on campus." Resits only for previous Newcastle applicants, one grade higher (AAA becomes A*AA).

## Liverpool: Medicine and Surgery MBChB (A100)
- https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/courses/medicine-and-surgery-mbchb — "Medicine and Surgery MBChB | Courses | University
  of Liverpool". "UCAS code A100", "A level: AAA", "5 years", start "September 2027", "Apply by: 15 October 2026".
- "AAA to include Chemistry together with either Biology, Physics or Mathematics and a third academic subject.
  Alternatively A*AB also accepted but the A* A grades must include Chemistry together with either Biology, Physics
  or Maths; and a B grade required in the third academic subject." "A levels in three subjects taken at one sitting,
  usually after 2 years of study". "Alternative offer conditions may apply."
- GCSE: "9 GCSEs attained by the end of Year 11 which must include English Language, Mathematic, Biology and
  Chemistry (or Core & Additional Science, combined or triple science) at minimum grade B/6. A minimum score of 15
  points from the best 9 GCSEs or equivalents (where A*/A/7/8/9 = 2 points; B/6 = 1 points)."
- "Non-graduate applicants applying to the A100 programme must sit the UCAT admissions test."
- Interview: not described on the course page. Contextual offer: not printed.

## Exeter: BMBS Medicine (A100)
- https://www.exeter.ac.uk/undergraduate-degrees/bmbs-medicine/ — "BMBS Medicine | 5-Year Medical Degree | University
  of Exeter". "UCAS code A100", "Duration 5 years", "Entry year 2027".
- "Entry requirements for 2027 entry (typical offer)": "A-Level A*AA A in Chemistry and A in Biology". "GCSE B or 6
  English Language". "Contextual Offer A-Level: AAB ... Specific subject requirements must still be achieved where
  stated above." "NB General Studies is not included in any offer."
- "All applicants are required to have taken either the UCAT Test or the GAMSAT Test." "Candidates applying with
  predicted or achieved A levels will also be required to sit the University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT)."
- "The interview style will be Multiple Mini Interview (MMI)." Interviews December to March.
- Deadline: "The deadline for applications to UCAS is 15 October." (no year printed)
