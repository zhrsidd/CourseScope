## Cambridge: Medicine, MB and BChir (A100)

- URL: https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/medicine (redirects to `/courses/medicine-mb-bchir`)
- Title: "Medicine, MB and BChir | Undergraduate Study"
- Key information: "UCAS code A100", "Course length MB, BChir 6 years full-time", "Start date October 2027",
  "Study at All Colleges, except Hughes Hall". First-degree students who finish the first 3 years graduate
  with a BA (Hons).
- "The entry requirements listed are for entry in 2027 or deferred entry in 2028."
- "Minimum offer level A level: A*A*A"
- "You can't apply to this course if you: will not be 18 years old on 1 November of year 1 ... have failed or
  been excluded from another medical school"
- College requirements: "To apply to any of our Colleges for Medicine you will need A Levels/IB Higher Levels
  (or the equivalent) in: Chemistry one or two other science or mathematics subject from: Mathematics Biology
  or Human Biology Physics Further Mathematics The number of science or mathematics subjects you need to have
  will depend on which College you apply to". "Colleges usually require A* in Chemistry."
- Chemistry plus ONE other: Fitzwilliam, Girton, Homerton, Murray Edwards, Newnham, Selwyn, Trinity Hall.
  Chemistry plus TWO others: every other College listed.
- UCAT: "You will need to take the University Clinical Aptitude Test." "For UCAT results, we will look at an
  applicant's overall cognitive subtest score. We will not be using the situational judgement score as part
  of our assessment for 2027 entry." "There is no minimum threshold for UCAT score."
- Interview: "We assess applicants through a mixture of academic record, UCAT and interviews."
- Contextual offer: not stated on the course page.
- Deadline: Cambridge's verified 15 October 2026 deadline for all courses already exists in the catalogue.
- Modelling: two pathways. With Chemistry plus two of the listed subjects, every College's subject rule is
  met, so this pathway can pass. With Chemistry plus one, only the seven named Colleges accept it, so that
  pathway carries a manual-review condition. Known limitation: Biology and Human Biology are listed as one
  option ("Biology or Human Biology"), but the count treats them as two subjects. A student taking both plus
  Chemistry could be counted as meeting the two-subject rule. This is recorded rather than hidden.

- UCAT: "You must sit the UCAT ... in 2026 before you submit your UCAS application." The academic assessment

- Interview: "We invite the top-scoring 800 applicants (approximately) to attend an assessment day
  (interview) either at Edinburgh Medical School or online."
- Deadline: "2027 entry UCAS deadline: 15 October 2026 (6:00pm GMT)".
